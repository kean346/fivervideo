## Gesture keyframes

Timing is manually synchronized, not automated motion tracking. Samples at 4 fps established movement windows; output keyframes are exact 30 fps positions.

- Clip 2 source 2.433 sec / output 9.833 sec: paceIT browser card rises beside the raised finger.
- Clip 2 source 4.233 sec / output 11.633 sec: hand sweeps inward; paceIT slides out while SA Family Travel slides in.
- Clip 3 source 1.833 sec / output 21.000 sec: Flow card appears during the dashboard introduction.
- Clip 3 source 6.067 sec / output 25.233 sec: Flow card exits.
- Clip 3 source 6.500 sec / output 25.667 sec: Northstar card appears next to the upward-pointing hand.

The founder's face stays unobstructed. All five recordings are retained in order. Only quiet clip boundaries are shortened; natural internal pauses remain to preserve delivery. The project inserts musical portfolio intervals to meet the 55–59-second duration without stretching speech.

## Speech and captions

Recognition was compared across Whisper small.en and medium.en. The medium.en transcript resolves the closing phrase as “a smarter process.” Proper names use the supplied branding and recognized “Kean.” Captions are phrase-grouped, timed from words, and constrained not to overlap. Original speech is used throughout; no cloned or generated voice is present.

## Portfolio and audio decisions

The four original PNGs replace all low-resolution thumbnail crops in the final timeline. Flow and Northstar are labelled demonstrations. Workflow sequences describe automation opportunities, not delivered integrations. No invented statistics, clients, results, or external contact information are added.

The actual paceIT and Flow recordings supply moving portfolio content. SAFT uses its original screenshot and its appearance in the paceIT recording; no separate SAFT screen recording is fabricated. Music and transition effects are synthesized from the included scripts rather than copied from third-party recordings. Speech has an 80 Hz high-pass, conservative denoising, loudness processing and the original timing.
